What is ACE Builders Hackathon? 

ACE Builders is a community of developers, innovators and AI enthusiasts who build applications on the Codebenders platform. The ACE Builders Hackathon 2026 is a free, global online hackathon by Codebenders. Over about 36 hours, you build a working application that solves one of five problem statements, using Codebenders and other tools allowed by the event rules. 

What does ACE stand for? 

ACE stands for Ambient Contextual Engineering.  

When does it happen? 

The hackathon runs on 21–22 November 2026. The opening session starts on Saturday, 21 November at 9:30 AM IST. The build window opens at 12:00 PM IST that day and closes at 11:59 PM IST on Sunday, 22 November. That gives you roughly 36 hours to build. 

Can I take part solo or in a team? 

You can take part solo or as a duo (a team of two). You will confirm your setup during the Problem Selection & Build Preparation session on Day 1, which runs from 9:30 AM to 12:00 PM IST.

The day opens at 9:30 AM IST with a welcome and a Codebenders introduction. A live demo follows, showing how to start a project on Codebenders, use the platform's key features, and follow best practices. 

When are the check-in and alert times? 

On Day 1, there is a build update at 1:00 PM, a final build and submission reminder at 6:00 PM, and a last alert at 11:00 PM (all IST). Submissions close at 11:59 PM IST. 

When are the problem statements revealed? 

All five problem statements are revealed live on Saturday, 21 November, from 11:00 to 11:30 AM IST. For each one, we explain the goal, the expected outcome and the evaluation criteria. A written one-pager for each will be shared right after the reveal. 

What tools can I use to build? 

You need to build with Codebenders IDE only.
